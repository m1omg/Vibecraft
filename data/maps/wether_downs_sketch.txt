Wether Downs (map id wether_downs, tileset gv): written-brief sketch, copied verbatim from
docs/design/slice-plan.md §1.3 (evidence of independent creation; M1 map_lookalike review).
1 character = 2 x 2 tiles = 4 m. Column and row headers are character indices. x = east, z = south.
Legend: ':' level 2, '.' level 1, ',' level 0, '/' ramp, '#' trees, '~' deep water, blank = map edge,
'1'/'2' main halls, '$' Gold Seam, 'n' natural hall site, 'g' green camp (Clamp / Turnip Patch),
'w' Woodward's Rest, 'h' Collier's Hut, 'y' Charcoal Yard, 'd' Dewstone site, 'a' Drystone Fold,
'S' Weighstall + Smugglers' Den, '+' Hale Spring + Crow Field, 'q' Cairn Gate, 'r' Standing Ring,
'k' knoll (Beacon Cairn site), 'i' Coaching Inn.
tools/maps/build_slice_map.py reads the 48 rows below the ---- line (exact positions come from the
slice-plan §1.4/§1.5 tables inside the script, not from the letters).
