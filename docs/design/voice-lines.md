## Generation (2026-10-20 pass)

- Service: Krea `elevenlabs/tts`, `model_id: eleven_v4`, preset voices only (no cloning, no voice design); no preset is
  shared between the factions. Cost: **2/27 CU per prompt character** (tags, spaces and newlines included), measured with
  free preflights; every request is in `tools/prompts/vo/vo_jobs.json` and `docs/ledger-pending/audio-vo.md`.
- One request = one category of one speaker (the two level-up lines share a request; an advisor's alerts share one),
  a single short speaker tag at the start (tags are billed like text), lines separated by a blank line.
- **Probe** (job `10192392`, Hodman What, 4 lines, three separators in one request): `[long pause]` gave a 0.99 s gap,
  `[pause]` 0.63 s, a bare blank line 0.60 s, against 0.39–0.46 s breaths inside two-sentence lines ("Mm? Mind the
  mortar.", "Thirty years. One Agnes."). Splitting uses the N−1 longest pauses for N expected lines, so the blank line
  is enough, and every exported line is transcribed (`tools/audio/vo_asr_check.py`, faster-whisper medium.en) and compared
  with its script text. Takes 1, 3 and 4 shipped (1.39 s, 1.38 s, 1.11 s after `atempo` ≤ 1.08); take 2 ran 2.4 s and was
  rejected (over the 1.4 s What cap even at `atempo` 1.15).
- Post (`tools/audio/vo_split.py`): split, trim (40 ms before, 70 ms after the voice), pauses inside a line tightened to
  0.15 s for acks (0.45 s for annoyed, 0.3 s otherwise), the speaker's post-chain, 5 ms / 20 ms fades, −18 LUFS
  integrated (ffmpeg `ebur128`) with a −1 dBFS limiter, `atempo` up to 1.15 when over the cap, else rejected; Ogg Vorbis
  mono `-q:a 5`, 44.1 kHz. The Sling-Cart brothers are two takes (two presets) of the same text, the younger fitted to the
  older's length and mixed 14 ms behind him, then post-processed as one line.
- Budget: the full slice script (371 lines) costs about 1,200 CU at this price; the 300 CU cap of this pass buys the P0
  acknowledgement set first (task tier a), then annoyed runs (tier b), then advisor alerts (tier c).
